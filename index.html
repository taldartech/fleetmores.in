CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions about TruckBill.

Everything you need to know about setting up TruckBill, printing stationery, and managing your daily transport accounts.

What is TruckBill and who is promoting it?

+
TruckBill is India's premier transport operations and billing ERP, and FleetMores is actively promoting it across India. It is built specifically for Indian transport contractors, fleet owners, and freight brokers to automate Lorry Receipts (bilty), trip dispatches, market vehicle settlements, fuel audits, and Section 194C TDS customer invoicing.

Can we print biltis on our existing pre-printed dot-matrix stationary?

+
Yes, 100%. TruckBill features a pixel-accurate print layout configurator. You can align margins, field positions, terms & conditions, and multi-part copy copies (Consignor, Consignee, Driver, Accounts) to match your exact existing dot-matrix stationery or print on standard A4/A5 laser paper.

How does TruckBill handle market lorry settlements and broker commissions?

+
TruckBill tracks customer freight rates and hired lorry hire rates simultaneously. When generating a lorry hire voucher, it automatically deducts petrol pump diesel slips, cash advances given to the driver, and Section 194C TDS. Your margin is locked upfront, ensuring brokers or vehicle owners are never overpaid.

Does TruckBill handle GST (RCM/FCM) and Section 194C TDS compliance?

+
Yes. TruckBill fully supports Reverse Charge Mechanism (RCM) and Forward Charge Mechanism (FCM) rules for Goods Transport Agencies (GTA). It automatically calculates Section 194C TDS (1% for individuals/HUF, 2% for companies/firms) and maintains records of Pan-10 declarations for exemption tracking.

How does the Proof of Delivery (POD) mobile upload work?

+
Drivers can upload a clear photo of the physically stamped receiving copy directly via smartphone or WhatsApp. Stamped PODs attach automatically to the corresponding digital LR, enabling your accounts team to submit customer invoices immediately without waiting weeks for the physical paper to travel back.

Can TruckBill manage multiple booking branches across India?

+
Yes. TruckBill supports multi-branch hierarchies. Booking branches in Delhi, transshipment hubs in Indore, and delivery offices in Bengaluru can generate their own series of LRs while giving Head Office centralized, real-time visibility over all dispatch registers, cashbooks, and customer ledgers.
