COMPREHENSIVE PLATFORM ARCHITECTURE

12 specialized engines built for Indian transport realities.

Every engine in TruckBill addresses an actual day-to-day bottleneck in freight operations — from dot-matrix LR printing to petrol pump reconciliation and broker margin lock.

01 • CORE BILLING
📄

Digital LR & Bilty Engine

Generate professional, error-free Lorry Receipts in seconds. Aligns pixel-accurately with your pre-printed dot-matrix stationary, captures multi-item goods with HSN codes, and sends instant PDFs to parties on WhatsApp.

Dot-Matrix Formats Multi-Copy Bilty WhatsApp Sharing
02 • DISPATCH
🚛

Trip Dispatch & Allocation

Assign own fleet, attached trucks, or market broker vehicles with verified driver loading slips. Verify driver DL and truck fitness before loading to eliminate border clearance delays.

Loading Slips Driver DL Check Market Vehicles
03 • BROKER KHATA
🤝

Market Lorry Settlement

Keep customer freight rate and hired lorry rate distinct. Automatically deduct diesel slip advances, cash vouchers, hamali, and Section 194C TDS before settling the broker's balance.

Dual-Rate Vouchers Advance Deductions Commission Lock
04 • TAX & BILLING
🧾

GST Invoicing & TDS 194C

Combine 1 or 50 biltis into a clean corporate tax invoice with detention charges. Auto-calculates 1% or 2% Section 194C TDS, manages RCM/FCM GST rules, and syncs GSTR-1 returns.

Multi-LR Invoicing 1% / 2% TDS Auto Detention Charges
05 • EXPENSE CONTROL
⛽

Diesel & FASTag Audit

Record petrol pump credit slips against specific trips. Reconcile monthly fuel pump statements from IOCL, BPCL, HPCL, and audit FASTag toll debits against estimated route mileage to prevent fuel theft.

Pump Reconciliation FASTag Toll Audit Mileage Control
06 • CASH FLOW
📸

Mobile POD Receiving Capture

Drivers upload photos of physically stamped POD receiving copies directly from their phone or via WhatsApp. Stamped POD instantly unblocks invoice submission to corporate consignors.

Instant WhatsApp Upload 15-Day Faster Billing Receiving Stamped
07 • LEDGER
📊

Transport Double-Entry Khata

Party ledgers, supplier broker khata, driver passbooks, and petrol pump registers linked automatically to trip dispatch. No need to re-enter data in external accounting tools.

Party Ledger Driver Passbook Zero Duplicate Entry

Vehicle-Wise Monthly Net P&L

Discover the exact financial performance of every commercial vehicle. Track freight revenue, diesel cost, maintenance, driver bata, tyre wear, and truck EMI every single month.

Per-Truck P&L Maintenance Tracking Tyre Cost per KM
09 • DRIVER SETTLEMENT

Driver Trip Passbook

Maintain complete trip settlement transparency. Driver trip allowances, route advances, toll reimbursements, and balance pay settled without disputes.

Driver Passbook Advance Tracking Zero Dispute Pay

Fitness Alerts National Permit Insurance Expiry
